    dkoepsell in Announcements · started 17 October 2026 · 0 replies · last activity 17 October 2026 · reply without GitHub · reply on GitHub

    Welcome to the Legal Ontologies Foundry

    The Foundry is building open, interoperable legal ontologies on Basic Formal Ontology (BFO), modeled on the OBO Foundry. Everything here is a draft for discussion, and input from anyone working on law, ontology, or legal information systems is welcome, especially before the founding coordinators' next meeting.

    Good places to start: - Read the four draft design patterns: https://legal-ontologies-foundry.github.io/odp/ - Introduce yourself in this thread: who you are, and what brings you here - Pick up a starter task: issues labeled "good first issue" - Ask anything in Q&A; no question is too basic

    David Koepsell, William Mandrick, and Barry Smith
