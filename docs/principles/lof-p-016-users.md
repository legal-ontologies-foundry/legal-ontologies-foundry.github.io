# LOF-P-016: Documented use

!!! warning "Draft for ratification"
    Part of the founding draft of the [LOF principles](index.md).

## Summary

The ontology has documented users, or a credible plan for use.

## Purpose

Ontologies built for no one tend to drift from real needs. Documented use shows that the ontology solves an actual problem and gives maintainers a community to consult.

## Recommendations and requirements

1. By the time of admission to *accepted*, the registry entry lists at least one independent use in its `usages` field (a dataset, application, project, or publication).
2. At *draft* and *candidate* status, a planned use suffices.

## Implementation

Record usages in the ontology's registry metadata file.

## Examples

See the LOF ontology template.

## Criteria for review

Listed usages exist and are independent of the maintainers where possible.

**Automated check:** Registry schema validation of `usages`.
