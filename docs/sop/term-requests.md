# SOP: Term requests

Anyone may request a new term, or a change to an existing term, in any member
ontology.

1. Open an issue in the ontology's tracker (`https://w3id.org/lof/{id}/tracker`)
   using the **New term request** form. Give a proposed label, definition,
   parent class, source, and an example of use.
2. A maintainer responds within 30 days (LOF-P-014), either adding the term,
   pointing to an existing term, or explaining why it is out of scope.
3. If the term belongs in a different member ontology, the maintainer
   transfers the issue there.
