# Working groups

Working groups are appointed by the Operations Committee. During the founding
phase, the committee members may lead working groups themselves. Each group
has a lead, who reports to the committee.

## Technical Working Group

Maintains the Foundry's infrastructure: this website, the registry and its
schema, the `w3id.org/lof` redirects, the ontology repository template, and
the [automated checks](../policy/automated-checks.md). Evaluates tooling
(ROBOT, the Ontology Development Kit, reasoners).

## Editorial Working Group

Conducts ontology reviews under the [review SOP](../sop/review.md), mediates
overlap disputes (LOF-P-009), maintains the design pattern series (LOF-ODP),
and proposes amendments to the principles.

## Outreach Working Group

Handles training materials, the newsletter or announcements, and liaison with
related initiatives: the OBO Foundry, the Industrial Ontologies Foundry, the
Common Core Ontologies community, and legal-informatics standards bodies such
