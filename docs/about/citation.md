# Citing the Foundry

## Citing a member ontology

Cite the specific ontology and release you used, following the citation in
its README, and give its version IRI. For example:

> TODO Author(s). *LOF-ODP-001: TODO pattern name*, version 0.1.0.
> Legal Ontologies Foundry. https://w3id.org/lof/odp-001/releases/0.1.0/odp-001.owl

## Citing the Foundry

Until a Foundry paper is published, cite the website:

> Legal Ontologies Foundry. https://w3id.org/lof/ (accessed YYYY-MM-DD).

When a founding paper appears, it will be listed here.
