# Code of Conduct

The Legal Ontologies Foundry brings together philosophers, lawyers, computer
scientists, and practitioners who disagree, often productively, about
foundational questions. This code exists to keep that disagreement useful.

## Expected conduct

- Argue about ideas, not people. Criticism of a model, definition, or
  principle is welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before concluding that someone
  is wrong.
- Credit the work of others (see LOF-P-018).
- Keep discussion relevant to the forum it is in.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption of
discussion, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in
any Foundry space: repositories, issue trackers, Discussions, meetings, or
mailing lists.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to any Operations Committee member. Reports are
handled confidentially. The committee may warn, temporarily restrict, or
remove participants who violate this code.
