## Intent

A great deal of law is about who occupies which legal role: judge, trustee,
executor, guardian, tenant, director. Roles are acquired and lost through
legal acts (appointments, contracts, removals, resignations), and they are
specified by legal content (the statute creating an office, the lease clause
defining the tenant's position). This pattern models legal roles as BFO roles
and connects them to the acts that confer and terminate them, and to the
content that specifies them.

## Competency questions

1. Which legal roles does this person or organization currently bear?
2. Which legal act conferred this role, and under what legal content?
3. Which legal act terminated this role?
4. Which legal content specifies what this role consists in?
5. Which of a person's legal roles arose by operation of law rather than by a
   conferring act?

## Solution

### Classes

| Label | IRI | BFO parent | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| legal role | `ODP002_0001000` | role | A role that inheres in a person or organization in virtue of some legal act or legal content, and that is realized in processes having legal significance. |
| conferred legal role | `ODP002_0001001` | legal role (defined) | A legal role that was brought into existence by some legal act. |
| role conferral act | `ODP002_0001002` | legal act (defined) | A legal act that brings some legal role into existence in some bearer. |
| role termination act | `ODP002_0001003` | legal act (defined) | A legal act that brings some legal role to an end. |

### Relations

| Label | IRI | Domain | Range | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| confers | `ODP002_0001100` | legal act | legal role | A relation between a legal act and a legal role that comes into existence through that act. |
| conferred by | `ODP002_0001101` | legal role | legal act | Inverse of *confers*. |
| terminates | `ODP002_0001102` | legal act | legal role | A relation between a legal act and a legal role that ceases to exist through that act. |
| specified by | `ODP002_0001103` | legal role | legal content | A relation between a legal role and legal content that specifies what the role consists in. |

The pattern reuses BFO *inheres in* (BFO:0000197) between a role and its
bearer, and BFO *realizes* (BFO:0000055) between a process and the role it
realizes.

### Axioms (Manchester syntax)

```
legal role  SubClassOf  role and (inheres in some material entity)
conferred legal role  EquivalentTo  legal role and (conferred by some legal act)
role conferral act    EquivalentTo  legal act and (confers some legal role)
role termination act  EquivalentTo  legal act and (terminates some legal role)
```

The last three are defined classes, so the reasoner classifies instances
automatically. In the worked example, the lease signing is asserted only to
be a contract formation that confers a tenant role; the reasoner infers that
it is a role conferral act, and that the tenant role is a conferred legal
role.

### Grounding rationale (LOF-P-004)

A person becomes a trustee, or ceases to be one, without any change in their
physical make-up. The role exists because of external circumstances (an
instrument, an appointment, a court order). That is the defining feature of
a BFO role, as opposed to a disposition, which is grounded in the bearer's
physical make-up.

## Example

```turtle
ex:anaTenantRole a odp2:0001000 ;        # legal role
    obo:BFO_0000197 ex:ana ;             # inheres in
    odp2:0001101 ex:leaseSigning ;       # conferred by
    odp2:0001103 ex:leaseTerms .         # specified by
```

Inferred: `ex:anaTenantRole` is a *conferred legal role*; `ex:leaseSigning`
is a *role conferral act*.

## Alignment with legal standards (LOF-P-015)

Compatible with role modeling in the Common Core Ontologies and with the
agent-role distinction in LKIF-Core, but grounded directly in BFO *role*.
Akoma Ntoso's `TLCRole` and `TLCPerson` references can be mapped to
*legal role* and its bearer.

## Consequences and limitations

- Roles arising by operation of law (a parent's guardianship of a newborn)
  are *legal roles* but not *conferred legal roles*. The pattern does not yet
  model what brings them into existence.
- The pattern does not state that the bearer of a conferred role participates
  in the conferring act. That is often true (a signing) and sometimes false
  (an appointment by letter), so it is left to domain ontologies.
- Offices that persist through changes of holder (the office of Chief Justice,
  as opposed to a particular person's role as Chief Justice) are not modeled.
  This is the most significant gap.

## Open questions for the founding coordinators

1. **Offices.** Should the Foundry model an office as something distinct from
   the roles of its successive holders? If so, is an office a generically
   dependent continuant specified by legal content, of which each holder's
   role is a concretization?
2. Should *legal role* be required to be *specified by* some legal content,
   making the link to the law mandatory rather than optional?
3. How should roles borne by organizations be treated, given that BFO has no
   settled account of organizations?
