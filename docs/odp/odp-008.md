## Intent

Every legal system needs some way to settle, for a particular case, what the
law requires, and to make that settlement stick. Hart called this the rule of
adjudication. The patterns so far model the law as made (ODP-001), as held
(ODP-003), as triggered (ODP-004), as changed (ODP-005), and as ranked
(ODP-006). They do not model the law as *applied*: the act in which a court
or tribunal finds the facts, decides what the law requires, and disposes of
the case.

Three features of adjudication need their own treatment.

- **Legal facts are not brute facts.** Once a court finds that the tenant did
  not pay April's rent, that finding binds the parties (res judicata, issue
  estoppel) even if the rent was in fact paid. The duty to vacate that the
  judgment generates rests on the finding, not on the non-payment. A legal
  knowledge base that records only what happened cannot explain why the law
  says what it does about a decided case; one that records only findings
  cannot report where the two diverge.
- **A decision gives rise to content of two kinds.** The ruling governs the
  parties (particular content). Its holding, the rule on which the case was
  decided, may also govern later cases (general content), but only in orders
  that make holdings binding. Whether a holding is law is a fact about the
  order, not about the holding.
- **Decisions relate to decisions.** Appeals affirm or reverse; later courts
  follow, distinguish, or overrule. Overruling is a change in the law, and so
  should connect to the amendment machinery of ODP-005.

## Competency questions

1. Which adjudicative act decided this case, who decided it, and in exercise
   of what power?
2. On what findings of fact does this position or ruling rest, and which
   recorded occurrents do those findings concern?
3. Which positions were in issue in this case, and which new positions did
   the ruling generate?
4. What holding was this case decided on, and is it binding precedent in its
   legal order?
5. Which later decisions followed, distinguished, or overruled this holding,
   and which reviewing decisions affirmed or reversed this ruling?
6. Which positions rest on a finding about an occurrent that is recorded
   elsewhere in the knowledge base as not having occurred?

Questions 1 to 5 are answered by SPARQL over the reasoned graph; question 6
by a SHACL shape, since it compares a finding with independent records.

## Solution

### Axioms (Manchester syntax)

```
adjudicative act  SubClassOf  legal act and (gives rise to some ruling)
finding of fact   SubClassOf  particular legal content and (arises from some adjudicative act)
ruling            SubClassOf  particular legal content and (arises from some adjudicative act)
ruling DisjointWith finding of fact
holding           SubClassOf  generically dependent continuant and (holding of some adjudicative act)
binding precedent EquivalentTo holding and legal content
binding precedent SubClassOf  general legal content
adjudicator role  EquivalentTo legal role and (has realization some adjudicative act)
adjudicative power EquivalentTo power and (exercised in some adjudicative act)
decisional practice EquivalentTo norm-generating practice and (has occurrent part some adjudicative act)

overrules SubPropertyOf amends                     # LOF-ODP-005
affirms, reverses SubPropertyOf reviews
```

*Rests on* also applies to legal content, such as a ruling resting on a finding (the OWL file declares no domain). *Gives rise to*, *arises from*, *general* and *particular legal content*, and
*norm-generating practice* are terms proposed for LOF-ODP-001 0.3.0. *Legal role*
is LOF-ODP-002; *power* and *exercised in* are LOF-ODP-003; *amends* is
LOF-ODP-005; *has realization* and *has occurrent part* are BFO.

### Grounding rationale (LOF-P-004)

- **Adjudicative act as legal act.** Delivering a judgment is a process with
  participants that brings legal content into being. It is a legal act in the
  sense of ODP-001, so it inherits everything that pattern and ODP-007 say
  about legal acts: authorization, membership by chain, and nullity (a
  judgment given without jurisdiction is a void act).
- **Findings and rulings as particular legal content.** A finding is
  information that binds, and it binds whether or not it is true; that is
  what separates it from a description of what happened. It is therefore
  legal content, a generically dependent continuant, borne by the judgment,
  the court record, and every authorized copy. It is particular because it
  governs these parties in this case.
- **Holding as a generically dependent continuant, and precedent by
  assertion.** Whether a holding is law depends on the order. Rather than
  build one tradition into the pattern (LOF-P-010), *holding* is not a
  subclass of legal content. A holding asserted to be legal content is
  classified as a *binding precedent*, which is general legal content, and,
  with ODP-007, belongs to the order under whose content the deciding court
  acted. This is the same device ODP-006 uses for authoritative
  determinations.
- **No new primitives for roles and powers.** *Adjudicator role* and
  *adjudicative power* are defined from ODP-002 and ODP-003, so their BFO
  category follows ODP-003: since 0.2.0, legal positions are BFO roles.

## Example

Northside sues Ana for possession in the justice court; the judgment is
affirmed on appeal, and the appellate court's holding is binding in the
state's courts. Excerpt:

```turtle
ex:evictionJudgment a odp8:0001000 ;          # adjudicative act
    obo:BFO_0000057 ex:judgeJones , ex:ana , ex:northside ;
    obo:BFO_0000055 ex:jonesJudgeRole ;       # realizes
    odp1:0001102 ex:tenantCode ;              # performed under
    odp1:0001100 ex:possessionRuling , ex:nonpaymentFinding ;
    odp1:0001111 ex:danNotice ;               # declares void (ODP-001 0.3.0 proposal)
    odp8:0001102 ex:anaAprilRentDuty .        # determines

ex:nonpaymentFinding a odp8:0001001 ;         # finding of fact
    odp8:0001101 ex:aprilPaymentWindow .      # finds

ex:anaDutyToVacate a odp3:0001002 ;           # duty
    odp3:0001101 ex:possessionRuling ;        # grounded in
    odp4:0001100 ex:evictionJudgment ;        # generated by
    odp8:0001100 ex:nonpaymentFinding .       # rests on

ex:appealDecision a odp8:0001000 ;
    odp8:0001108 ex:evictionJudgment ;        # affirms
    odp1:0001100 ex:appealRuling , ex:noticeHolding .
ex:noticeHolding a odp8:0001003 , odp1:0001001 ;   # holding, asserted to be law
    odp8:0001107 ex:appealDecision .          # holding of

ex:overrulingCase a odp8:0001000 ;
    odp8:0001105 ex:oldHolding .              # overrules
```

Inferred by HermiT, under both ODP-003 options:

| Entity | Inferred |
|---|---|
| Judge Jones's role | *adjudicator role* |
| the justice court's power to decide eviction suits | *adjudicative power* |
| the notice holding | *binding precedent*, *general legal content*, belongs to the state order (ODP-007) |
| the judgment for possession | belongs to the state order (ODP-007) |
| the older holding | *binding precedent* (only a binding precedent can be overruled) |
| the overruling decision | *amending act* (ODP-005) |
| the eviction judgment | *declaration of nullity* (ODP-001 0.3.0 proposal), of Dan's notice |
| Ana's duty to vacate | *fully grounded legal position* (ODP-004) |
| a line of two decisions giving rise to a rule on deposits | *decisional practice* |

Akoma Ntoso marks up the parts of a judgment document; this pattern models
what those parts are. The two are complementary in the way ODP-001 and the
FRBR levels are. Mappings to be verified against current releases before
publication as SSSOM.

## Consequences and limitations

- **Findings that something did not happen.** OWL individuals are taken to
  exist, so an occurrent the court found to have happened, but which did not,
  should not be recorded as an individual. The finding then stands with no
  *finds* link, and the positions resting on it are still grounded. Where an
  occurrent the finding concerns *is* recorded, and an independent record
  shows it went otherwise, the SHACL shape for question 6 reports the
  divergence.
- **Merger in judgment.** In many systems the claim sued on merges into the
  judgment and ceases to exist; a judgment debt replaces it. The pattern
  records this as a position *determined* by the act and a new position
  *generated by* it. Whether the old position ended is recorded with ODP-005.
  The law itself treats this as the end of one position and the start of
  another, which fits ODP-003 0.2.0's positions as roles with *succeeds*.
- **No court hierarchy.** Which courts' holdings bind which courts is a
  structural fact about an order. The pattern records that a holding binds
  (by asserting it to be legal content), not which courts it binds. A court
  hierarchy pattern could be added later.
- **Interpretation is not modeled.** How a holding is extracted from a
  judgment, and whether two courts read it the same way, is outside the
  pattern. Competing readings can be recorded as distinct holdings of the
  same act.
- **Juries and lay adjudicators.** A jury's verdict is a finding of fact and,
  in criminal cases, a ruling; jurors bear adjudicator roles. Nothing more is
  needed.
- **Jurisdiction.** Checked against the forms below (LOF-P-010):

| Form | How it is expressed |
|---|---|
| Common-law stare decisis | holdings asserted to be legal content; *follows*, *distinguishes*, *overrules* |
| Civil-law case law without binding precedent | holdings not asserted to be legal content; a *decisional practice* gives rise to general content |
| Cassation | *reviews*, *reverses*, with remand left to domain ontologies |
| Arbitration | an *adjudicative act* whose adjudicative power is grounded in the arbitration agreement (contract formation, ODP-001) |
| Administrative tribunals | adjudicative acts performed under the enabling statute |
| Religious and customary courts | adjudicative acts in an order *recognized* or *embedded* by the state order (ODP-007) |
| Constitutional review | a *ruling* that is also an authoritative precedence determination (ODP-006); annulment ex tunc by ODP-005 |

## Open questions for the founding coordinators

1. **Must every adjudicative act give rise to a ruling?** The axiom says so.
   A dismissal for want of jurisdiction is still a ruling; a hung jury
   produces none. Is the hung jury an adjudicative act?
2. **Holding as legal content by assertion.** This mirrors ODP-006. An
   alternative is to relate a holding to the orders in which it binds
   (*binding in*), making precedent relative to an order explicitly. Which
   is preferred?
3. **Overruling and retroactivity.** Overruling is a subproperty of
   *amends*, and retroactivity is recorded by ODP-005 *applies during*.
   Prospective overruling (as practised by some courts) fits. Should the
   pattern say anything more?
4. **Findings by non-adjudicative bodies.** Administrative agencies,
   coroners, and registrars make binding findings outside adjudication.
   Should *finding of fact* be generalized to any legal act, with
   adjudication as one source?
5. **Court hierarchy.** Should the Foundry add a pattern for the structure of
   courts (which bodies' holdings bind which, and appellate routes), or leave
   it to domain ontologies?
