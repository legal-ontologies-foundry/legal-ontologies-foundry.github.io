# Design Patterns

Ontology design patterns (ODPs) are small, reusable modeling solutions to
recurring problems in representing legal reality: for example, how an
obligation depends on its bearer, how a legal role is conferred and revoked,
or how a legal act brings an institutional entity into being. Member
ontologies are expected to use Foundry patterns where they apply, or to
document why they depart from them (see principle [LOF-P-004](../principles/lof-p-004-grounding.md)).
