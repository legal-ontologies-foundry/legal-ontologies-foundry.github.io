Models legal orders as systems of legal content with their own recognition criteria, so that validity is membership in an order: content is valid in an order when it meets that order's criteria, directly or through embedding and recognition of other orders. Nullity is relative to an order (*void in*). The full specification is on the [pattern page](../odp/odp-007.md).
