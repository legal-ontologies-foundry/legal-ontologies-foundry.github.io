Models legal personality as conferred by a legal order on natural persons, organizations, and other entities (rivers, idols), and capacity as the power to perform legal acts, with voidable acts for incapacity. A SHACL shape reports positions held by entities without legal personality. The full specification is on the [pattern page](../odp/odp-009.md).
