Relates items of legal content by particularization and derogation (substitutive or blocking), locates conflict between the legal positions they ground and lifts it to the content, and records precedence determinations (lex superior, specialis, posterior, primacy of an overriding legal order) with their source; only determinations that are themselves legal content make one content prevail over another. SHACL shapes report unresolved, contested, and unauthoritatively resolved conflicts. The full specification is on the [pattern page](../odp/odp-006.md).
